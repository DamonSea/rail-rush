# Rail Rush

A neon space lane-runner you play in the browser. Switch between three tracks, jump and slide past ships, mines and laser gates, grab coins for pogo sticks and jetpacks, and stay ahead of the big alien chasing you.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any browser (desktop or phone).

## Controls
- **Swipe ← / →** or **A / D / arrow keys**: switch tracks
- **Swipe ↑** or **W / Space / ↑**: jump
- **Swipe ↓** or **S / ↓**: slide

## Features
- 15 heroes, crates with hats, glasses, jetpacks and pogo sticks (3D previews)
- Starting powers: Double Jump, Indestructible, Flying Cape
- UFO boss and the Ray Blaster
- Neon planet double-speed boost
- 4 background music tracks
