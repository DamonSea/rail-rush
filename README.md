# Rail Rush

A neon space lane-runner you play in the browser. Switch between three tracks, jump and slide past ships, mines and laser gates, grab coins for pogo sticks and jetpacks, and stay ahead of the big alien chasing you.

## Controls
- **Swipe ← / →** or **A / D / arrow keys**: switch tracks
- **Swipe ↑** or **W / Space / ↑**: jump
- **Swipe ↓** or **S / ↓**: slide
- **⏸ button**, **P** or **Esc**: pause

### PS5 controller (or any Bluetooth/USB gamepad)
- **D-pad / left stick ← →** (or **L1**): switch tracks
- **✕** or **↑**: jump · **○** or **↓**: slide
- **□ / R1 / R2**: fire the Ray Blaster at UFOs
- **✕** or **Options**: start / run again
- **Options**: pause (↑↓ + ✕ in the pause menu, ○ to resume)
- The controller rumbles when you get hit

## Features
- Pause menu (resume with a 3-2-1 countdown, restart, main menu, sound settings); auto-pauses if you switch tabs or your controller disconnects
- Option to turn off the scary game-over screen (Sound tab)
- 15 heroes, crates with hats, glasses, jetpacks and pogo sticks (3D previews)
- Starting powers: Double Jump, Indestructible, Flying Cape
- UFO boss and the Ray Blaster
- Neon planet double-speed boost
- 7 background music tracks, including 3 pulsing sci-fi synth tracks
